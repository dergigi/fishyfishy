# Watch together: documentary links

Checked 2026-09-19. These are direct video links, not searches or embedded players. YouTube oEmbed confirmed each title and publisher. Links may become unavailable or region-restricted; oEmbed metadata is not a guarantee of playback in every region. Selection uses publisher descriptions and supporting pages, not a claim that every frame has been reviewed or that the videos are age-rated. Parents can watch together. Some films include hunting, feeding or reproduction. All selections are English; captions and translated tracks depend on YouTube.

Every guide entry has a link. Short documentary clips and science films are preferred where there is a relevant established publisher. Species without a selected close match use the explicitly labelled Mediterranean coastal-habitat film. That film is not claimed to feature every linked species or to have been filmed in Madeira. Related-animal labels prevent facts about tropical relatives being presented as facts about the local animal.
